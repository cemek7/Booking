# Booka revenue intake visual review

Reviewed the public Revenue Pilot and Missed Revenue Report application pages at desktop (1440 px) and mobile (390 px) widths.

## Captures

- `revenue-pilot-desktop.png`
- `revenue-pilot-mobile.png`
- `missed-revenue-report-desktop.png`
- `missed-revenue-report-mobile.png`

## Result

- Both pages preserve their content hierarchy and form usability at both widths.
- The form collapses from two columns to one column on mobile without clipping or horizontal overflow.
- Labels, consent language, channel controls, and primary actions remain readable and reachable.
- The beauty vertical is the default while the form retains cross-vertical choices.
- No customer-conversation upload or free-text collection control is present.

The mobile captures include the existing global cookie-consent banner and the Next.js development indicator. Those overlays are not part of the revenue-intake pages and the development indicator is not present in production.
