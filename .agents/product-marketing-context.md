# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-08-29*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Booka turns WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries into booked and paying customers.
**What it does:** Booka answers questions, recommends services and products, checks availability, follows up, books customers, helps collect payment, escalates exceptions, and reports commercial outcomes. It lets customers continue using the messaging channels they already know.
**Product category:** AI Revenue Front Desk
**Product type:** Multi-tenant SaaS with managed onboarding, usage-based operating costs, and an optional managed-service tier
**Business model:** All-inclusive monthly subscription with fair-use allowances and transparent opt-in overages. Launch prices: Core ₦15k/month, Revenue Front Desk ₦45k/month, Growth ₦85k/month, Managed Revenue Operations ₦250k+/month.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Service businesses that receive meaningful WhatsApp or Instagram enquiries. Cross-vertical from launch; beauty businesses are the default demonstration and first proof cohort. Initial vertical clusters are beauty, hospitality, and clinics.
**Decision-makers:** Business owners and operators responsible for revenue, bookings, customer experience, and staff workload.
**Primary use case:** Convert existing messaging enquiries into more bookings, sales, deposits, and repeat business without requiring customers to adopt a new app.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Respond to every viable enquiry and give it a clear next step.
- Recommend, sell, book, and help collect payment in one conversation.
- Recover stalled opportunities and support repeat business.
**Use cases:**
- Beauty: price questions, service recommendations, stylist/slot alternatives, deposits, products, reminders, rebooking.
- Hospitality: reservations, availability alternatives, occasion add-ons, deposits, confirmations, guest follow-up.
- Clinics: non-clinical consultation routing, appointments, reminders, recall, and safe escalation.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Businesses already receive demand through WhatsApp and Instagram but lose money through slow responses, weak recommendations, booking friction, unavailable first choices, and missing follow-up.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Booking software waits for customers to navigate a separate booking flow.
- Generic chatbots answer questions but do not reliably sell, book, collect deposits, recover, and report.
- Staff-only inbox handling is inconsistent outside operating hours and during busy periods.
**What it costs them:** Lost bookings and sales, empty capacity, no-shows, abandoned enquiries, staff time, and unmeasured opportunities.
**Emotional tension:** Owners know customers are messaging but cannot see how much business disappears inside the inbox.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** AI receptionists and conversational-booking tools — often stop at support or scheduling.
**Secondary:** Booking platforms, social inboxes, and CRM tools — organize work but still require staff to convert conversations.
**Indirect:** Receptionists, social-media managers, spreadsheets, and the owner's personal WhatsApp — familiar but inconsistent, difficult to scale, and difficult to measure.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Booking and selling in the same conversation
- Native operation across WhatsApp and Instagram, with channel-specific lifecycle boundaries
- Recommendations, alternatives, deposits, payment links, reminders, recovery, and rebooking
- Human takeover and escalation
- Processed, influenced, and recovered revenue reporting
**How we do it differently:** Booka operates across the whole commercial conversation rather than handing the customer a booking link after answering a question.
**Why that's better:** Customers stay in familiar channels, fewer opportunities die between enquiry and transaction, and owners can connect activity to outcomes.
**Why customers choose us:** Booka makes existing enquiries more valuable without asking the business or its customers to replace WhatsApp and Instagram.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “We already use WhatsApp/Instagram.” | Booka improves the value of those existing enquiries; it does not replace the channels. |
| “AI may say the wrong thing.” | Booka is grounded in configured services, prices, availability, and policies, with human escalation and takeover. |
| “How do I know it makes money?” | The pilot reports verified Booka-linked bookings, sales, deposits, follow-ups, and recovered opportunities with explicit attribution labels. |
| “I don't want surprise usage charges.” | Plans include fair usage, send alerts before limits, require opt-in for overages, and cap uncontrolled campaigns. |

**Anti-persona:** Businesses with negligible enquiry volume, inaccurate availability/pricing, no staff escalation path, no willingness to record outcomes, or a requirement for unrestricted medical advice or spam messaging.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Missed DMs, delayed replies, repetitive questions, abandoned prospects, empty slots, no-shows, and unclear staff performance.
**Pull:** Always-available conversational handling tied to bookings, sales, payment, recovery, and reporting.
**Habit:** Owners and staff already manage customers from personal or shared messaging accounts.
**Anxiety:** Losing control of the customer voice, incorrect AI replies, Meta account risk, complicated setup, and surprise charges.

**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Processed revenue | A transaction completed through a Booka-managed journey |
| Influenced revenue | Revenue where Booka assisted before conversion |
| Recovered revenue | Revenue from an identifiable stalled or dormant opportunity converted after recovery |
| Verified outcome | A booking, order, deposit, payment, or recovery supported by product or merchant-confirmed evidence |
| Active pilot day | A day after setup and successful live-channel verification |
