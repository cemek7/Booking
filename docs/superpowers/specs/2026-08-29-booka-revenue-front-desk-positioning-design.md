# Booka Revenue Front Desk — Positioning, Offer, Proof, and Pricing Design

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Approved in brainstorming; ready for implementation planning after written review
**Scope:** Booka positioning, website copy architecture, 14-day pilot, Missed Revenue Report, proof policy, pricing architecture, and measurement requirements.

## 1. Decision

Booka remains a cross-vertical product for service businesses. Beauty businesses are the default demonstration, onboarding preset, first pilot cohort, and first source of case-study evidence.

Booka's category is **AI Revenue Front Desk**, not AI booking software or a generic chatbot.

Primary promise:

> **Booka turns your WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries into booked and paying customers.**

Campaign line:

> **Turn conversations into customers.**

Business-facing description:

> Booka handles customer conversations from enquiry to sale, booking and repeat business across WhatsApp and Instagram.

The product and reporting sequence is:

> **Answer → Recommend → Sell → Book → Pay → Follow up → Retain → Report**

## 2. Product Reality and Boundaries

The positioning is grounded in existing product foundations: tenant-scoped WhatsApp and Instagram conversations, service and product recommendations, availability, bookings, deposits, payment links, retail orders, reminders, rebooking nudges, lead-recovery campaigns, human takeover, operational campaign records, front-desk events, and outcome-attribution records.

The following boundaries prevent the message from outrunning the product:

- **Instagram** is presented primarily as an enquiry-capture and in-window conversion channel. Booka does not promise unrestricted proactive Instagram follow-up.
- **WhatsApp** carries the broader lifecycle: qualification, booking, deposits, reminders, recovery, rebooking, and compliant initiated messaging.
- Booka **helps collect payment** through configured payment links and deposits. It is not described as holding merchant customer funds.
- Clinics receive non-clinical routing, scheduling, reminders, and safe escalation. Booka does not diagnose or provide clinical advice.
- Cross-channel identity is not assumed. A WhatsApp contact and an Instagram contact are separate until the product can safely connect them.
- "Additional revenue" is not claimed without a defensible baseline or counterfactual. Initial reporting uses processed, influenced, and recovered revenue definitions.

## 3. Audience Architecture

### 3.1 Shared audience

Service businesses that already receive meaningful customer enquiries through WhatsApp or Instagram and lose commercial opportunities through slow replies, weak recommendations, unavailable first choices, inconsistent follow-up, booking friction, or fragmented staff handling.

The decision-maker is normally the owner or operator responsible for revenue, bookings, staff workload, and customer experience.

### 3.2 Default wedge: beauty

The first proof cohort prioritizes salons, spas, barbers, beauty studios, and similar businesses because their workflows naturally combine social discovery, repetitive price questions, recommendations, availability, deposits, appointments, complementary sales, no-show exposure, and repeat cycles.

Default beauty example:

> A customer asks, "How much are knotless braids?" Booka recommends a suitable option, gives accurate prices, offers available times or staff, requests the configured deposit, and confirms the booking.

### 3.3 Other visible verticals

- **Hospitality:** availability, reservations, occasion add-ons, deposits, confirmations, and guest follow-up.
- **Clinics:** appropriate consultation routing, appointment availability, reminders, and escalation for sensitive or clinical questions.

The homepage leads with the shared money problem. Vertical modules demonstrate how the same revenue-front-desk system adapts without presenting three separate products.

## 4. Message Hierarchy

### 4.1 Homepage hero

**Eyebrow**

> AI Revenue Front Desk for service businesses

**Headline**

> Turn your WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries into booked and paying customers.

**Body**

> Booka answers customer questions, recommends the right service or product, checks availability, follows up, books customers and helps collect payment—while your team steps in when human judgement is needed.

**Primary CTA**

> Apply for the 14-Day Revenue Pilot

**Secondary CTA**

> Get a Missed Revenue Report

"Apply" is deliberate: Booka must qualify enquiry volume and operating readiness before accepting the revenue-pilot obligation.

### 4.2 Sales hook

> We're not asking you to replace WhatsApp or Instagram. We're making the enquiries you already receive worth more money.

Supporting sales line:

> Booka answers, recommends, follows up, sells and books even when your team is busy, asleep or helping the customer in front of them.

### 4.3 Money-problem framework

1. **Capture:** respond to enquiries automatically, day or night.
2. **Convert:** move each viable enquiry toward a booking, sale, lost opportunity, or deliberate no.
3. **Recover:** offer alternatives and follow up when the first path stalls.
4. **Grow:** rebook, reactivate, cross-sell, retain, and help fill weak capacity.

Capture and Convert are the initial proof obligation. Recover differentiates the product. Grow becomes more prominent after longer measurement windows produce evidence.

## 5. Booka 14-Day Revenue Pilot

### 5.1 Offer

> Connect Booka to your WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries. For 14 active days, Booka will answer questions, recommend services and products, qualify customers, follow up, book appointments and help close sales.
>
> If Booka does not produce at least one verified booking, sale, deposit or recovered opportunity attributable to a Booka conversation, there is no obligation to continue.

The pilot begins only after configuration and a successful live-channel test.

### 5.2 Included work

- Managed channel connection
- Service/product catalogue, price, availability, FAQ, and policy setup
- Booking, sales, deposit, and payment-link configuration
- Follow-up and escalation rules
- Conversation monitoring and calibration
- End-of-pilot performance report

### 5.3 Eligibility

A pilot applicant must have:

- sufficient weekly enquiry volume to make the test meaningful;
- accurate services/products, prices, policies, and availability;
- an operational WhatsApp or Instagram business account that can be connected;
- a staff contact for escalations;
- willingness to record completed bookings, offline sales, and relevant outcomes.

The precise minimum enquiry threshold is calibrated from the first cohort. It is an internal qualification rule until validated.

### 5.4 Success definition

A qualifying outcome must be verifiable and connected to a Booka journey:

- booking created;
- sale/order created;
- deposit or payment completed;
- stalled opportunity converted following a Booka recovery action.

The pilot does not promise a specific Naira return, conversion lift, repeat-booking lift, or no-show reduction.

## 6. Missed Revenue Report

The report is the lead magnet, qualification tool, baseline instrument, and sales mechanism.

Initial delivery is concierge-assisted rather than a fully automated historical-inbox ingestion product.

Headline:

> **How much business is sitting unanswered in your inbox?**

Description:

> We review a sample of your WhatsApp and Instagram enquiry process and identify unanswered messages, missing follow-ups, availability dead ends, abandoned buying conversations, and missed recommendation opportunities.

The report includes:

- enquiries reviewed;
- unanswered or materially delayed replies;
- conversations without a clear next step;
- availability enquiries that ended without alternatives;
- prospects who disappeared without follow-up;
- missed service/product recommendation opportunities;
- an estimated recoverable opportunity range.

The estimate is a range based on the supplied sample, merchant-provided average transaction values, and visible outcomes. It is labelled as an opportunity estimate, not guaranteed revenue.

Sensitive customer content must be consented, minimized, and redacted where practical. The first version does not require broad historical account access.

## 7. Proof and Attribution Policy

Unsupported numerical proof is removed from public pages, including unverified claims such as "40% no-show reduction" or "₦3.6m revenue recovered."

Before case studies exist, the website uses capability proof:

- automated enquiry handling;
- booking and selling in one conversation;
- deposits and payment links;
- reminders, recovery, and rebooking workflows;
- human takeover;
- measurable event and outcome tracking.

Revenue reporting uses three non-overlapping labels:

- **Processed revenue:** a transaction completed through a Booka-managed journey.
- **Influenced revenue:** Booka recommended, answered, followed up, or otherwise assisted before conversion.
- **Recovered revenue:** a previously stalled, unavailable, cancelled, abandoned, or dormant opportunity converted following an identifiable recovery action.

Booka does not combine all three into an inflated "generated revenue" total. Reports show the attribution window, event source, and data completeness. Offline outcomes are not counted as verified until the merchant records or confirms them.

## 8. Pricing and Billing

### 8.1 Customer-facing model

Booka uses an all-inclusive subscription with fair-use allowances and transparent, opt-in overages. Tokens and internal credits are not central marketing concepts.

Customers receive usage alerts before any overage. High-volume initiated campaigns require approval. A hard cap prevents surprise bills and uncontrolled provider cost.

### 8.2 Launch plans

| Plan | Price | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| **Booka Core** | **₦15,000/month** | Booking and sales workspace for teams that manage most conversations themselves |
| **Booka Revenue Front Desk** | **₦45,000/month** | Flagship conversational sales, booking, follow-up, and escalation product |
| **Booka Growth** | **₦85,000/month** | Higher-volume recovery, reactivation, campaigns, attribution, and support |
| **Managed Revenue Operations** | **₦250,000+/month** | Human + AI operations, active quality monitoring, custom workflows, and dedicated optimization |

### 8.3 Cost allocation

- Subscription pricing covers the workspace, standard automation, included AI/messaging usage, and the service level assigned to the plan.
- AI inference is tracked internally using exact provider cost where available, with allowance for provider funding fees, foreign-exchange movement, fallbacks, and margin.
- Customer-initiated service conversations and business-initiated Meta messages are tracked separately because their cost behavior differs.
- Campaign allowances are separate from ordinary enquiry handling; unlimited marketing or reactivation messaging is never promised.
- Payment-processor transaction charges are merchant expenses unless a later commercial agreement explicitly states otherwise.
- Managed onboarding, configuration, conversation QA, support, retries, and exception handling are included in unit-economics measurement because they are expected to dominate early cost more than raw tokens.

### 8.4 Allowance calibration

Exact published allowances are intentionally deferred until pilot telemetry establishes:

- conversations per merchant;
- messages and AI calls per conversation;
- tokens and actual provider cost per successful journey;
- initiated Meta messages per customer;
- failure/retry rates;
- onboarding and monthly support hours;
- cost per verified commercial outcome.

During the first cohort, usage is monitored internally, merchants receive no surprise overage, and numerical fair-use bands remain part of the signed pilot/order terms rather than unvalidated public claims.

## 10. Measurement Requirements

The implementation must support a pilot report containing:

- enquiries received by channel;
- automated and human-handled conversations;
- qualified opportunities;
- bookings created;
- orders/sales created;
- deposits/payments completed;
- follow-ups scheduled and sent;
- recovered opportunities;
- processed, influenced, and recovered revenue;
- escalations and unresolved outcomes;
- Booka provider cost and gross contribution internally.

Every money figure must carry a source, attribution type, period, and verification status.

## 11. Rollout Sequence

1. Correct public positioning and remove unsupported proof.
2. Add pilot and Missed Revenue Report application surfaces.
3. Establish pilot qualification, onboarding, and outcome-confirmation operations.
4. Run the beauty-led first cohort while accepting qualified cross-vertical applicants.
5. Measure true AI, Meta, onboarding, and support cost.
6. Publish fair-use bands and overages from observed distributions.
7. Publish the first verified case studies.
8. Expand vertical-specific demonstrations and operating presets based on evidence.

## 12. Out of Scope

- Automated unrestricted ingestion of historical WhatsApp or Instagram inboxes
- Unqualified revenue guarantees
- Cross-channel identity merging
- Unrestricted proactive Instagram messaging
- Clinical diagnosis or medical advice
- Unlimited promotional messaging
- Percentage-of-revenue pricing in the first cohort
- Public numerical allowances before pilot calibration

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The strategy is implemented correctly when:

- public copy consistently describes Booka as an AI Revenue Front Desk;
- the primary promise includes both booking and selling;
- beauty leads demonstrations without excluding other service verticals;
- unsupported proof numbers are absent;
- the Revenue Pilot has eligibility, start, success, and measurement rules;
- the Missed Revenue Report uses ranges and explicit assumptions;
- pricing shows the approved four-plan architecture;
- usage and overage language is understandable without tokens or credits;
- Instagram, payment, clinic, and attribution boundaries are stated honestly;
- the product can produce a source-backed pilot outcome report.
